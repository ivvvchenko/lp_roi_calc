Where it is stored

Form submissions are delivered by email through a form-handling service (FormSubmit) to my Gmail inbox. Your browser keeps the calculator numbers for the current tab session and the campaign tags for up to 90 days in local storage, so a later visit is attributed correctly. You can clear them by clearing this site's data in your browser.

How long

I keep your request for as long as we are in touch about the plan, and no longer than 12 months, unless we start working together.
